## 3. ML Models

### Model A – Revenue Forecasting (per product × quarter)
- **Algorithm:** Gradient Boosted Trees (XGBoost / LightGBM) + Seasonal ARIMA for trend
- **Target:** `revenue_next_quarter`
- **Features:** historical revenue (lag 1-8), product lifecycle stage, budget gap %, macro indicators
- **Output:** Forecast band (P10 / P50 / P90) per product

### Model B – Deal Prioritization Score (0-100)
- **Algorithm:** Logistic Regression / XGBoost classifier → probability of closing
- **Target:** `will_close (1) / will_not_close (0)`
- **Features:** deal_value, days_in_stage, stage_velocity, AE win-rate, product win-rate, account size, budget_gap_pct, loss-reason embeddings
- **Output:** `close_probability` → rank all open deals

### Model C – Product Lifecycle Stage Prediction
- **Algorithm:** Multi-class classifier (XGBoost) with ordinal-aware loss
- **Target:** Next lifecycle stage (1→6)
- **Use:** Flag deals stuck in "Negotiate" too long, or "Development reports" that won't convert

### Model D – Deal-Loss Root-Cause Clustering
- **Algorithm:** TF-IDF + UMAP + HDBSCAN on `loss_reason` text (CRM notes, call transcripts)
- **Output:** Clusters like *"product not working"*, *"budget cut"*, *"chose competitor"*
- **Action:** Route "product not working" clusters to product/engineering team

### Model E – Budget Gap Alerting
- **Algorithm:** Simple threshold + anomaly detection (Isolation Forest) on `gap_pct`
- **Output:** List of products > X% below target → trigger pipeline push
