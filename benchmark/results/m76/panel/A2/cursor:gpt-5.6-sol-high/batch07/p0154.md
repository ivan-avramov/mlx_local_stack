- Feature engineering vs. representation learning

**Resources**
- *Introduction to Machine Learning* – Andrew Ng (Coursera, 2024 update)

**Resources**

## Key Principles

1. **Build from scratch first, then use libraries.** Implement backprop, a CNN, and a Transformer by hand before reaching for PyTorch modules.
2. **Ship projects.** A GitHub portfolio of 8–12 well-documented projects beats 50 courses.
3. **Read papers, not just tutorials.** You don't need to read every paper, but reading 30–50 core ones builds real intuition.
4. **Teach what you learn.** Write a blog post or explain it to someone — it exposes gaps.
5. **Don't skip the math.** Intuition matters, but you'll hit a wall without gradient calculus and linear algebra.
