## Prompt
Two fair, 6-sided dice are thrown.  What is the probability that the product of the two numbers is a multiple of 5?  Express your answer as a common fraction.

## Response A
The product of the two dice values is a multiple of 5 if and only if at least one of the dice shows a 5, since 5 is the only number from 1 to 6 that is divisible by 5.

Total possible outcomes:

\[
6 \times 6 = 36
\]

Count the favorable outcomes:

- First die is 5: \(6\) outcomes
- Second die is 5: \(6\) outcomes
- Both dice are 5: counted twice, so subtract \(1\)

Thus, the number of favorable outcomes is

\[
6 + 6 - 1 = 11
\]

Therefore, the probability is

\[
\frac{11}{36}
\]
