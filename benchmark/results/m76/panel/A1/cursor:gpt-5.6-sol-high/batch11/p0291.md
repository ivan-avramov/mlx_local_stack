## Prompt
Let $f(x) = x^3 + 3x^2 + 1.$  There exist real numbers $a \neq 0$ and $b,$ such that
\[f(x) - f(a) = (x - a)^2 (x - b).\]Enter the ordered pair $(a,b).$

## Response A
We are given

\[
f(x)=x^3+3x^2+1
\]

and we need real numbers \(a\neq 0\) and \(b\) such that

\[
f(x)-f(a)=(x-a)^2(x-b).
\]

Since the right-hand side has a factor \((x-a)^2\), the polynomial \(f(x)-f(a)\) has a double root at \(x=a\). Therefore,

\[
f'(a)=0.
\]

Compute the derivative:

\[
f'(x)=3x^2+6x=3x(x+2).
\]

Thus

\[
f'(a)=0 \implies 3a(a+2)=0.
\]

So

\[
a=0 \quad \text{or} \quad a=-2.
\]

Since \(a\neq 0\), we must have

\[
a=-2.
\]

Now compute \(f(a)\):

\[
f(-2)=(-2)^3+3(-2)^2+1=-8+12+1=5.
\]

So

\[
f(x)-f(-2)=x^3+3x^2+1-5=x^3+3x^2-4.
\]

We want

\[
x^3+3x^2-4=(x+2)^2(x-b).
\]

Expand the right-hand side:

\[
(x+2)^2(x-b)=(x^2+4x+4)(x-b).
\]

\[
=x^3+4x^2+4x-bx^2-4bx-4b.
\]

\[
=x^3+(4-b)x^2+(4-4b)x-4b.
\]

Compare coefficients with

\[
x^3+3x^2+0x-4.
\]

From the \(x^2\)-coefficient:

\[
4-b=3 \implies b=1.
\]

Check the other coefficients:

\[
4-4b=4-4=0,
\]

and

\[
-4b=-4.
\]

Both match. Therefore,

\[
(a,b)=(-2,1).
\]

\boxed{(-2,1)}
