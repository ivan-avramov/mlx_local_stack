The set of points $(x,y,z)$ that satisfy

## Response A
# Finding the Angle Between Two Lines

## Step 1: Find the direction vector of Line 1

Set $2x = 3y = -z = t$. Then:

$$x = \frac{t}{2}, \quad y = \frac{t}{3}, \quad z = -t$$

A direction vector is $\vec{v}_1 = \left(\frac{1}{2},\, \frac{1}{3},\, -1\right)$. Scaling by 6:

$$\vec{v}_1 = (3,\; 2,\; -6)$$

## Step 2: Find the direction vector of Line 2

Set $6x = -y = -4z = t$. Then:

$$x = \frac{t}{6}, \quad y = -t, \quad z = -\frac{t}{4}$$

A direction vector is $\left(\frac{1}{6},\, -1,\, -\frac{1}{4}\right)$. Scaling by 12:

$$\vec{v}_2 = (2,\; -12,\; -3)$$

## Step 3: Compute the angle using the dot product

$$\vec{v}_1 \cdot \vec{v}_2 = (3)(2) + (2)(-12) + (-6)(-3) = 6 - 24 + 18 = 0$$

Since the dot product is **zero**, the direction vectors are perpendicular.

$$\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{v}_1 \cdot \vec{v}_2}{|\vec{v}_1|\,|\vec{v}_2|} = \frac{0}{|\vec{v}_1|\,|\vec{v}_2|} = 0$$

$$\theta = 90°$$
