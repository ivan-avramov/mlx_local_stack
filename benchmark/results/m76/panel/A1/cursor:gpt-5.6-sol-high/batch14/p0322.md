## Prompt
In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 17$, $AC = 8$, and $BC = 15$.  Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$.  Find the area of triangle $ACD$.

## Response A
Since

\[
AC^2+BC^2=8^2+15^2=64+225=289=17^2=AB^2,
\]

triangle \(ABC\) is a right triangle with right angle at \(C\), and \(AB\) is the hypotenuse.

The area of \(\triangle ABC\) is

\[
[ABC]=\frac12(8)(15)=60.
\]

Let \(CD=h\) be the altitude from \(C\) to \(AB\). Then

\[
[ABC]=\frac12(AB)(CD)=\frac12(17)h=60,
\]

so

\[
h=\frac{120}{17}.
\]

Now use the right-triangle projection relation:

\[
AC^2=AB\cdot AD.
\]

Thus

\[
AD=\frac{AC^2}{AB}=\frac{8^2}{17}=\frac{64}{17}.
\]

Triangle \(ACD\) has base \(AD\) and height \(CD\), so

\[
[ACD]=\frac12(AD)(CD)
=\frac12\left(\frac{64}{17}\right)\left(\frac{120}{17}\right).
\]

Therefore,

\[
[ACD]=\frac{3840}{289}.
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac{3840}{289}}
\]
