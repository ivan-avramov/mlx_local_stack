**PT ∥ QR** (both are horizontal lines).

The labeled angles are:
- **∠P = x°** (interior angle at P)
- **∠T = 2x°** (interior angle at T)
- **128°** at R is the **exterior** angle (between the extension of QR past R and segment RT)

## Finding x Using Parallel Lines

Since **PT ∥ QR**, the transversal **TR** creates same-side interior angles at T and R that sum to 180°.

The **interior** angle at R is:
$$180° - 128° = 52°$$

Applying the same-side interior angle theorem:
$$2x + 52 = 180$$
$$2x = 128$$
$$x = 64$$

## Finding ∠PQR

Now the transversal **QP** crosses the parallel lines PT and QR. The same-side interior angles at P and Q sum to 180°:

$$x + \angle PQR = 180°$$
$$64° + \angle PQR = 180°$$
$$\angle PQR = 116°$$

## Verification

Sum of all interior angles of the quadrilateral:
$$64° + 128° + 52° + 116° = 360° \checkmark$$
