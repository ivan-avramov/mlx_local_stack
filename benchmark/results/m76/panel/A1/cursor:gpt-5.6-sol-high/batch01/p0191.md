## Identifying the Triangle

From the diagram, the right angle is at **S**, and the hypotenuse **RT = 5**.

Since $\sin R = \frac{2}{5}$, and in a right triangle $\sin R = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$:

$$\frac{ST}{RT} = \frac{2}{5} \implies ST = 2$$

## Finding the Remaining Side

Using the Pythagorean theorem:

$$RS = \sqrt{RT^2 - ST^2} = \sqrt{5^2 - 2^2} = \sqrt{25 - 4} = \sqrt{21}$$

## Computing sin(T)

Angle $T$ is at vertex $T$. The side **opposite** angle $T$ is $RS$, and the hypotenuse is $RT$:

$$\sin T = \frac{RS}{RT} = \frac{\sqrt{21}}{5}$$
