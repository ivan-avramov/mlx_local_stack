## Prompt
Let $a,$ $b,$ $c,$ $d$ be positive real numbers such that $a + b + c + d = 1.$  Find the minimum value of
\[\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{4}{c} + \frac{16}{d}.\]

## Response A
We want to minimize

\[
\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac4c+\frac{16}{d}
\]

subject to

\[
a+b+c+d=1,
\]

where \(a,b,c,d>0\).

Use Cauchy-Schwarz in the form

\[
\left(\sum_{i=1}^4 \frac{p_i}{x_i}\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^4 x_i\right)
\ge
\left(\sum_{i=1}^4 \sqrt{p_i}\right)^2.
\]

Here take

\[
(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=(a,b,c,d)
\]

and

\[
(p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4)=(1,1,4,16).
\]

Since \(a+b+c+d=1\), we get

\[
\left(\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac4c+\frac{16}{d}\right)(1)
\ge
\left(\sqrt1+\sqrt1+\sqrt4+\sqrt{16}\right)^2.
\]

Thus

\[
\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac4c+\frac{16}{d}
\ge
(1+1+2+4)^2
=8^2
=64.
\]

Equality holds when

\[
\frac{a}{\sqrt1}=\frac{b}{\sqrt1}=\frac{c}{\sqrt4}=\frac{d}{\sqrt{16}},
\]

so

\[
a=b=\frac18,\qquad c=\frac14,\qquad d=\frac12.
\]

These values are positive and sum to \(1\), so the minimum is attained.

\[
\boxed{64}
\]
