## Setting Up the Projection Condition

The projection of a vector $\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$ onto $\mathbf{u} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is:

$$\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf{u}} \mathbf{v} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{u}}\,\mathbf{u}$$

## Finding When the Projection is Zero

For the projection to equal $\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$, the scalar coefficient must be zero:

$$\frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{u}} = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u} = 0$$

Computing the dot product:

$$\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = 2x + y = 0$$

## Writing in Slope-Intercept Form

$$y = -2x$$
