## Prompt
I have a bag with yellow and blue marbles in it. At the moment, the ratio of blue marbles to yellow marbles is 4:3. If I add 5 blue marbles and remove 3 yellow marbles, the ratio will be 7:3. How many blue marbles were in the bag before I added more?

## Response A
## Setting Up the Problem

Since the ratio of blue to yellow marbles is 4:3, I'll let:
- Blue marbles = 4x
- Yellow marbles = 3x

## Applying the Changes

After adding 5 blue marbles and removing 3 yellow marbles:
- Blue marbles = 4x + 5
- Yellow marbles = 3x − 3

The new ratio is 7:3, so:

$$\frac{4x + 5}{3x - 3} = \frac{7}{3}$$

## Solving the Equation

Cross-multiplying:

$$3(4x + 5) = 7(3x - 3)$$

$$12x + 15 = 21x - 21$$

$$15 + 21 = 21x - 12x$$

$$36 = 9x$$

$$x = 4$$

## Finding the Answer

Blue marbles = 4x = 4(4) = **16**

**Verification:** After the changes, blue = 16 + 5 = 21 and yellow = 12 − 3 = 9, giving a ratio of 21:9 = 7:3 ✓
