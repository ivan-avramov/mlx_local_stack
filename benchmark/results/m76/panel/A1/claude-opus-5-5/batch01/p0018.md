## Prompt
For some real numbers $a$ and $b$, the equation \[
8x^3 + 4ax^2 + 2bx + a = 0
\]has three distinct positive roots. If the sum of the base-2 logarithms of the roots is 5, what is the value of $a$?

## Response A
# Finding the Value of $a$

## Setting Up Vieta's Formulas

First, I divide the cubic by 8 to get it in monic form:

$$x^3 + \frac{a}{2}x^2 + \frac{b}{4}x + \frac{a}{8} = 0$$

Let the three distinct positive roots be $r_1, r_2, r_3$. By Vieta's formulas:

$$r_1 r_2 r_3 = -\frac{a}{8}$$

## Using the Logarithm Condition

The sum of the base-2 logarithms is 5:

$$\log_2 r_1 + \log_2 r_2 + \log_2 r_3 = 5$$

By the logarithm product rule, this becomes:

$$\log_2(r_1 r_2 r_3) = 5 \implies r_1 r_2 r_3 = 2^5 = 32$$

## Solving for $a$

Equating the two expressions for the product of roots:

$$-\frac{a}{8} = 32$$

$$a = -256$$

## Verification

With $a = -256$: the sum of roots is $-a/2 = 128 > 0$ ✓, and the product is $-a/8 = 32 > 0$ ✓, both consistent with three positive roots.
