## Prompt
Find all $p$ which satisfy both the inequalities $0\ge 54p-144$ and $0>12-20p$. Express your answer in interval notation, reducing any fractions in your answer.

## Response A
We need to satisfy both inequalities:

\[
0 \ge 54p - 144
\]

Rewrite as:

\[
54p - 144 \le 0
\]

Add 144 to both sides:

\[
54p \le 144
\]

Divide by 54:

\[
p \le \frac{144}{54}
\]

Reduce the fraction:

\[
\frac{144}{54}=\frac{8}{3}
\]

So:

\[
p \le \frac{8}{3}
\]

Now solve the second inequality:

\[
0 > 12 - 20p
\]

Rewrite as:

\[
12 - 20p < 0
\]

Subtract 12 from both sides:

\[
-20p < -12
\]

Divide by \(-20\), which reverses the inequality:

\[
p > \frac{12}{20}
\]

Reduce the fraction:

\[
\frac{12}{20}=\frac{3}{5}
\]

So:

\[
p > \frac{3}{5}
\]

Combining both conditions:

\[
\frac{3}{5} < p \le \frac{8}{3}
\]

In interval notation:

\[
\boxed{\left(\frac{3}{5}, \frac{8}{3}\right]}
\]
