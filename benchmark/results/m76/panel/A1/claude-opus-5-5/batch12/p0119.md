## Prompt
A group of $N$ students, where $N < 50$, is on a field trip. If their teacher puts them in groups of 8, the last group has 5 students. If their teacher instead puts them in groups of 6, the last group has 3 students. What is the sum of all possible values of $N$?

## Response A
We are given two remainder conditions:

- When grouped into groups of 8, the last group has 5 students:

\[
N \equiv 5 \pmod{8}
\]

- When grouped into groups of 6, the last group has 3 students:

\[
N \equiv 3 \pmod{6}
\]

We also know:

\[
N < 50
\]

Since the moduli are 8 and 6, their least common multiple is:

\[
\operatorname{lcm}(8,6)=24
\]

So we look for numbers less than 50 that satisfy both congruences.

From

\[
N \equiv 5 \pmod{8}
\]

the possible residues modulo 24 are:

\[
5,\ 13,\ 21
\]

Now check which of these is congruent to 3 modulo 6:

\[
5 \equiv 5 \pmod{6}
\]

\[
13 \equiv 1 \pmod{6}
\]

\[
21 \equiv 3 \pmod{6}
\]

Thus,

\[
N \equiv 21 \pmod{24}
\]

The positive values of \(N\) less than 50 are:

\[
N = 21,\ 45
\]

Their sum is:

\[
21+45=66
\]

\[
\boxed{66}
\]
