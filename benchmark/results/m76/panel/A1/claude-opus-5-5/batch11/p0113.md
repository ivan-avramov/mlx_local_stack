## Prompt
The complex numbers $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$, and $\alpha_4$ are the four distinct roots of the equation $x^4+2x^3+2=0$. Determine the unordered set \[
\{\alpha_1\alpha_2 + \alpha_3\alpha_4, \alpha_1\alpha_3 + \alpha_2\alpha_4, \alpha_1\alpha_4 + \alpha_2\alpha_3\}.
\]

## Response A
Let \(a_i=\alpha_i\). By Vieta’s formulas for

\[
x^4+2x^3+2=0,
\]

we have

\[
e_1=\sum a_i=-2,\qquad e_2=\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j=0,
\]
\[
e_3=\sum_{i<j<k}a_ia_ja_k=0,\qquad e_4=a_1a_2a_3a_4=2.
\]

Define

\[
A=a_1a_2+a_3a_4,\quad B=a_1a_3+a_2a_4,\quad C=a_1a_4+a_2a_3.
\]

We need the unordered set \(\{A,B,C\}\).

First,

\[
A+B+C=e_2=0.
\]

Next,

\[
AB+AC+BC=e_1e_3-4e_4=-8.
\]

Also,

\[
ABC=e_1^2e_4-4e_2e_4+e_3^2=(-2)^2(2)=8.
\]

Thus \(A,B,C\) are the roots of

\[
t^3-(A+B+C)t^2+(AB+AC+BC)t-ABC=0,
\]

so

\[
t^3-8t-8=0.
\]

Factor:

\[
t^3-8t-8=(t+2)(t^2-2t-4).
\]

Hence

\[
t=-2,\qquad t=1\pm\sqrt5.
\]

Therefore the required unordered set is

\[
\boxed{\{-2,\;1+\sqrt5,\;1-\sqrt5\}}.
\]

## Response B
Let

\[
A=\alpha_1\alpha_2+\alpha_3\alpha_4,\qquad
B=\alpha_1\alpha_3+\alpha_2\alpha_4,\qquad
C=\alpha_1\alpha_4+\alpha_2\alpha_3.
\]

We want the unordered set \(\{A,B,C\}\).

By Vieta’s formulas for

\[
x^4+2x^3+2=0,
\]

the elementary symmetric sums of the roots are

\[
e_1=\sum \alpha_i=-2,\qquad
e_2=\sum_{i<j}\alpha_i\alpha_j=0,
\]
\[
e_3=\sum_{i<j<k}\alpha_i\alpha_j\alpha_k=0,\qquad
e_4=\alpha_1\alpha_2\alpha_3\alpha_4=2.
\]

---

### 1. Sum \(A+B+C\)

The sum \(A+B+C\) is exactly the sum of all six pairwise products:

\[
A+B+C=e_2=0.
\]

---

### 2. Compute some power sums of the roots

Let

\[
p_k=\alpha_1^k+\alpha_2^k+\alpha_3^k+\alpha_4^k.
\]

We have

\[
p_1=e_1=-2.
\]

Also,

\[
p_2=e_1^2-2e_2=(-2)^2-0=4.
\]

Using Newton’s identity,

\[
p_3=e_1p_2-e_2p_1+3e_3=(-2)(4)-0+0=-8.
\]

Since each root satisfies \(\alpha_i^4+2\alpha_i^3+2=0\), summing over all roots gives

\[
p_4+2p_3+8=0.
\]

Thus

\[
p_4=-2p_3-8=-2(-8)-8=8.
\]

---

### 3. Compute \(AB+BC+CA\)

Expanding \(AB+BC+CA\), the terms involving \(\alpha_1^2\) are

\[
\alpha_1^2(\alpha_2\alpha_3+\alpha_2\alpha_4+\alpha_3\alpha_4).
\]

Because \(e_2=0\),

\[
\alpha_2\alpha_3+\alpha_2\alpha_4+\alpha_3\alpha_4
=-\alpha_1(\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4).
\]

Since \(\alpha_2+\alpha_3+\alpha_4=e_1-\alpha_1=-2-\alpha_1\), this becomes

\[
-\alpha_1(-2-\alpha_1)=\alpha_1^2+2\alpha_1.
\]

Therefore, summing over all four roots,

\[
AB+BC+CA=\sum_{i=1}^4 \alpha_i^2(\alpha_i^2+2\alpha_i)
=p_4+2p_3.
\]

So

\[
AB+BC+CA=8+2(-8)=-8.
\]

---

### 4. Compute \(ABC\)

Expanding \(ABC\),

\[
ABC=e_4(\alpha_1^2+\alpha_2^2+\alpha_3^2+\alpha_4^2)
+(\alpha_1\alpha_2\alpha_3)^2
+(\alpha_1\alpha_2\alpha_4)^2
+(\alpha_1\alpha_3\alpha_4)^2
+(\alpha_2\alpha_3\alpha_4)^2.
\]

The first part is

\[
e_4p_2=2\cdot 4=8.
\]

For the remaining four terms, note that

\[
(\alpha_1\alpha_2\alpha_3)^2=e_4^2\cdot \frac1{\alpha_4^2},
\]

and similarly for the others. Hence

\[
(\alpha_1\alpha_2\alpha_3)^2+\cdots+(\alpha_2\alpha_3\alpha_4)^2
=e_4^2\sum_{i=1}^4\frac1{\alpha_i^2}.
\]

Now

\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\frac1{\alpha_i}=\frac{e_3}{e_4}=0,
\]

and

\[
\sum_{i<j}\frac1{\alpha_i\alpha_j}=\frac{e_2}{e_4}=0.
\]

Therefore,

\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\frac1{\alpha_i^2}
=\left(\sum_{i=1}^4\frac1{\alpha_i}\right)^2
-2\sum_{i<j}\frac1{\alpha_i\alpha_j}=0.
\]

Thus the remaining four terms contribute \(0\), and

\[
ABC=8.
\]

### 5. Form the cubic whose roots are \(A,B,C\)

The monic cubic with roots \(A,B,C\) is

\[
z^3-(A+B+C)z^2+(AB+BC+CA)z-ABC=0.
\]

Substituting the values found above,

\[
z^3-0z^2-8z-8=0,
\]

so

\[
z^3-8z-8=0.
\]

Factor:

\[
z^3-8z-8=(z+2)(z^2-2z-4).
\]

Thus the roots are

\[
z=-2,\qquad z=1\pm\sqrt5.
\]

Therefore,

\[
\boxed{\{-2,\;1+\sqrt5,\;1-\sqrt5\}}
\]
