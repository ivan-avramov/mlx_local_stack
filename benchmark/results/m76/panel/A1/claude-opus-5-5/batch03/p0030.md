## Prompt
Suppose $a$ and $b$ are different prime numbers greater than 2. How many whole-number divisors are there for the integer $a(2a+b)-2a^{2}+ab$?

## Response A
First simplify the expression:

\[
a(2a+b)-2a^2+ab
\]

Distribute \(a\):

\[
a(2a+b)=2a^2+ab
\]

So the expression becomes

\[
2a^2+ab-2a^2+ab=2ab
\]

Thus the integer is

\[
2ab
\]

Since \(a\) and \(b\) are different prime numbers greater than \(2\), they are odd primes and are distinct from \(2\). Therefore

\[
2ab=2^1a^1b^1
\]

is the prime factorization, where \(2\), \(a\), and \(b\) are three distinct primes.

The number of positive whole-number divisors is

\[
(1+1)(1+1)(1+1)=2\cdot 2\cdot 2=8
\]

So the answer is

\[
\boxed{8}
\]
