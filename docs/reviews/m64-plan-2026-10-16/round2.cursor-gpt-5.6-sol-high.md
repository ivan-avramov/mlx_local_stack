P2 — New or newly exposed problems

1. E2 declares per-language, per-session inference but applies Holm only over `2 picks × 2 contrasts`. That is not the family actually being interpreted.
2. “Censored-token lower bounds from server-side accounting” is unsupported: C154 records `tokens: null`, and the worker records no failed-request usage. `token_turn_gate.py` only preserves client-visible text/time evidence.
3. `egress = loopback only` cannot be established by C160 socket sampling. Sampling can miss short-lived connections and does not constrain shell-issued networking.
4. The proposed length-adjusted logistic model treats judge/order observations as independent and does not define clustered uncertainty, missing judges or order disagreement.
5. Web-off creates a new opencode scaffold identity. Golden parity must first cover the existing tg1 carrier, then separately validate the new deny-all carrier.
6. E1’s 1–2-hour bridge estimate is unjustified, especially when its anchor set deliberately includes C154-prone hour-long requests.
7. R1 conflicts with the checked-in record: `docs/handoff.md` says the chain is live, while C164 still makes it the M64 baseline. Acceptance of v2 does not silently amend those rulings.
8. R1’s archive name does not match the runner’s canonical `s2/incomplete/...` semantics, and reusing the same out-root would overwrite `chain.json` unless the complete prior attempt is moved first.

P3 — Remaining go blockers

- E1: blocked as an accepted methodology until the adoption gate has objective outcomes, epoch fields are enforced by `chain.json`/`validate_leg`/comparison code, and active chains cannot be retargeted.
- E2: blocked by the unresolved web treatment, unidentified C154 token endpoint, incoherent multiplicity family, absent concrete arm schedule, and incomplete subjective-analysis contract. At roughly 21–22 items per language, ±5pp equivalence will ordinarily be unreachable; the spec must either expand N or explicitly redefine M64 as an estimation screen.
- G1: blocked. `proc_guard.py` currently records a new readable PID-1 orphan diagnostically without making cleanup uncertain. Delaying unreadable reconciliation does not close that ownership escape.
- R1: conditionally reasonable, but blocked until the rc-2 state is verified, C164/handoff are formally amended, and the archive/new-invocation procedure is exact. The tg1 probe must not resume an aborted manifest.

P4 — Required edits to v2

1. E1: define `epoch_id`, per-tool snapshot schemas, `generation_identity_sha256`, `scoring_code_sha256`, bridge manifest and atomic adoption; comparison/reporting must refuse generation-identity mismatch.
2. E1: pre-register bridge red flags and outcomes. Contract failure means reject; gross-drift trigger means hold; passing permits only a new family and never claims equivalence.
3. E2: name operational scaffold performance as primary. Matched-budget capability is out of scope unless every client passes an accelerated timeout contract and can deliver the prescribed budget.
4. E2: require complete per-request server receipts before claiming lower tokens. Otherwise report identified bounds and mark token superiority “not established”; exclusion sensitivity cannot satisfy the gate.
5. E2: define four global primary claims by pick and reference contrast, each as an intersection across language/session cells; specify its combined equivalence test and then Holm over those four claims.
6. E2: require ≥80% simulated power or enlarge the corpus; otherwise remove equivalence from PASS and label the experiment a sensitivity estimate.
7. E2: publish the exact six-permutation block schedule, residual allocation, cache reset, warm-up and realized-schedule artifact.
8. E2: decide web policy before approval and enforce it, including shell networking, rather than merely sampling sockets.
9. E2: specify a code-quality rubric, pair-specific both-pass coverage, judge/order reconciliation, missing-judge rule and item-clustered length adjustment.
10. G1: preserve uncertainty for unreadable candidates that later become readable but remain ownership-unresolved; cover readable post-last-tick descendants, reparenting, vanished parents, PID reuse and retained descendants.
11. R1: amend C164 and handoff first; archive the entire failed attempt outside the active out-root; launch a new `chain s2 reload` invocation with the pinned identity; retain prior s1 and failed-attempt evidence as descriptive only.
