P6 — **New problems in v3**

1. **E1.2 can prohibit the intended experiment.** Generation identity includes adapter code and snapshot, so Pi and opencode necessarily differ. “Refuse a generation-identity … mismatch inside one family” must mean deviation from each arm’s registered identity, not equality across arms. Require one common `epoch_id` and a frozen mapping `arm_id → generation_identity_sha256`; comparisons explicitly authorize the registered treatment differences.

2. **G1 does not explicitly cover unreadable → readable but unattributed.** Readability resolves an inspection failure, not ownership. This matters because [`tick()`](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py:404) deletes recovered-readable records, and [`_reconcile_unreadable()`](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py:800) skips descendant reconciliation for a readable same-identity root. V3 must forbid clearing retained obligations solely on that transition.

3. **R1 describes a partial move as moving the whole failed attempt.** Leaving s1 active is sensible; the archival record should nevertheless include a hashed copy of s1 and any referenced evidence needed to reconstruct the original attempt. Distinguish the complete preserved record from the files removed to make room for replacement s2.

4. **Several “per-request on-wire” controls are not wire-observable.** M50, corpus integrity, ambient instructions and child-process deadlines need lifecycle/configuration/process proofs. Require a proof matrix assigning each invariant to its actual enforcement and evidence boundary.

These are wording/design requirements, not reasons to demand implementation before approving the specification-writing phase.

P7 — **G2 may remain separate from G1, with one boundary condition.**

The separation is supported by the code:

- The [module contract](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py:1) explicitly acknowledges detached-process attribution gaps and diagnostic-only same-UID orphans.
- In [`cleanup()`](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py:874), tracked identities are killed; path-attributed processes set uncertainty; a young, otherwise unattributed PID-1 orphan merely gets an orphan record.
- That orphan branch does not set `uncertain`; [final uncertainty](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py:948) comes from unresolved reconciliation and other explicit failures.

Therefore, the readable, never-recorded G2 residual predates G1. A bounded reconciliation proposal need not solve every pre-existing ownership gap. Cursor’s requirement that this gap categorically block G1 is too broad.

**The boundary condition:** G1 must preserve uncertainty for roots or descendants already recorded through unreadability until their obligations are affirmatively resolved. It cannot convert those records into reasonless G2 orphans merely because inspection recovers. That would weaken the very reconciliation G1 modifies.

Also replace “closing it needs ownership capture at spawn” with “closing it requires a separately validated ownership mechanism; spawn-time containment/tagging is one candidate.” Process-group/session tagging alone is not yet a demonstrated solution for descendants that detach.

P8 — **Minimum edits for approval**

- **E1 methodology:** Replace family-wide generation-identity equality with the frozen per-arm mapping described in P6. Keep the common epoch requirement and existing transport prohibition.
- **E2 specification to write:** Require the proof matrix; require block sizes and randomized allocation of permitted Williams sequences to be specified. Require adversarial enforcement tests covering shell children, alternate destinations and proxy attempts before accepting the proposed sandbox boundary. The operator can choose web policy during specification preparation; no build approval follows.
- **G1 proposal:** Add: “Recovered readability alone discharges neither an ownership-unresolved recorded root nor any recorded descendant obligation. Resolve each as gone, protected/pre-existing, or attributed and reconciled; otherwise remain uncertain at the global deadline.” Add an explicit unreadable→readable, reparented, path-negative regression case. Preserve existing overflow and unknown-identity fail-closed behavior.
- **R1 conditional:** Require verified abort/exit state and process termination, reconcile the handoff, and explicitly amend C164 before launch. Preserve a complete hashed attempt record including retained s1; move conflicting active outputs only after archive verification. Use the full computed identity and full executable invocation in the launch checklist.

The runner’s [`write_json()`](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/chains/c147/run_tg1_chain.py:213) confirms the overwrite concern, and its [argument parser](mlx_local_stack/benchmark/chains/c147/run_tg1_chain.py:721) supports `chain s2 reload --probe-code-sha … --out-root …`. Those facts support the proposed restart shape; they do not verify the local attempt’s state.
