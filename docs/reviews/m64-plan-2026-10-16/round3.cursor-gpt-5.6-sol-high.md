P4 — New or remaining v3 problems

1. E1’s new token-ratio bridge red flag is undefined when either snapshot produces incomplete/censored usage. Missing evaluability must itself mean HOLD, and the ratio’s aggregation level must be stated.
2. `epoch_id` needs a canonical manifest encoding including schema version, tool names, and ordered snapshot hashes; “hash of shas” is not uniquely reproducible as written.
3. E2 says Holm separately across three families without defining overall confirmatory error control or the joint M64 PASS rule.
4. E2 lacks mandatory sandbox conformance positives: router allowed, other loopback ports denied, external networking denied, and denial inherited by model-controlled shell children.
5. The checked-in M64 queue still prescribes the old baseline and PASS rule. E2 approval must explicitly authorize amendment of `docs/PLAN.md`; R1 separately requires C164/handoff amendment.
6. R1 says the whole failed attempt moves out, then leaves s1 in place without including it in the hashed inventory. That is internally inconsistent.
7. G2’s suggested process-group/session tagging is not sufficient by itself: a daemon can call `setsid()`. G2 should leave the ownership mechanism open rather than imply that tagging closes it.

P5 — G2 versus G1

Separation is acceptable and should not block G1. The implementation deliberately distinguishes two mechanisms:

```1:5:benchmark/bench/proc_guard.py
"""M62 best-effort process attribution, memory monitoring and durable artifacts.

A fork that detaches and leaves scratch between samples can escape attribution.
New same-uid orphans are diagnostic only and are never killed without attribution.
"""
```

A readable young PID-1 orphan is recorded without setting `uncertain`:

Conversely, `_note_unreadable` and `_reconcile_unreadable` retain recorded obligations and make a still-unreadable process uncertain; `test_unreadable_order_for_an_eligible_process_makes_cleanup_uncertain` verifies that behavior. `test_detached_shell_pid_reuse_and_rapid_orphan` verifies the separate diagnostic-only residual.

Thus G1 fixes bounded convergence for known unreadable obligations. G2 concerns missing ownership evidence altogether and needs a different architecture. Make G1’s “post-last-tick descendant” test explicitly mean “descendant of a recorded unreadable root”; otherwise its wording accidentally reabsorbs G2.
