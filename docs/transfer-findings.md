**Provisional draft, 2026-09-13. Independent cold factual review completed; pending M43 upstream-runtime validation.**
This report uses the completed M40/M41 record and earlier mechanism investigations. It reports no H200/B200 measurements and predicts no GPU speedup or model ranking. ## Mechanisms worth testing on another backend

| Transferable hypothesis or method | Supporting local evidence | What must be remeasured on H200/B200 |
|---|---|---|
| **Capacity and latency are separate constraints.** A configuration can fit while long-prompt prefill dominates response time. | M41's 41.10 GB peak and 1987.85-second prefill at the largest rung, above. Attention-cost growth is a plausible contributor; the rows do not isolate it. | Peak device allocation during real prefill, allocator reservation, scratch/KV lifetimes, TTFT and decode separately, at the intended batch and concurrency. Use the backend's appropriate memory instrument; do not carry over the 46 GB policy threshold or MLX metric. |
| **Predictor acceptance alone cannot decide profitability.** Accepted tokens must repay draft, verify, rollback, and synchronization costs. | M40 measured Math500 decode ratios of 1.93 [1.90,1.96] for `Qwen3.8-27B-Fable-Distill-OptiQ-4.5bpw-mixed` and 1.78 [1.74,1.82] for `Qwen3.8-27B-mlx-uniform-4bit`. M29's `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-4bit` retained only a 1.18× screen result despite high acceptance. [First-pick Math500 comparison](../benchmark/results/Qwen3.8-27B-Fable-Distill-OptiQ-4.5bpw-mixed/math500.m40on.vs.m37med.json); [second-pick Math500 comparison](../benchmark/results/Qwen3.8-27B-mlx-uniform-4bit/math500.m40on.vs.m37med.json); [M29 H1 round-profile entry](campaign-results.md) | Paired ON/OFF quality, output lengths, task latency, acceptance and per-round cost by context, workload, batch size and backend kernel. Do not extrapolate the local multipliers. |
| **A packaging defect can masquerade as a poor predictor.** Validate counters against a known-positive before interpreting zero acceptance. | Repairing seven normalization vectors restored 84.5% acceptance in the three-task M36 speed screen for `Qwen3.8-27B-Fable-Distill-OptiQ-4.5bpw-mixed`; that screen preceded the quality study. [M36 speed-screen artifact](../benchmark/results/Qwen3.8-27B-Fable-Distill-OptiQ-4.5bpw-mixed/mtp.m36corrected.json); [M36 repair and original-control entries, 2026-09-09/10](campaign-results.md) | Check sidecar identity, normalization convention, tensor layout, load path and counters independently of target-model quality. A successful load is insufficient. |
| **Cache reuse depends on routing and retention.** A configured cache may never receive the request. | The old serving path bypassed APC for session-cached requests; APC counters stayed zero while session caching reduced repeated-prefix work. Historical APC allocation also caused OOM. [APC investigation](serving-path.md#apc-is-off-everywhere-benchmarks-and-the-daily-driver) | Trace real request dispatch, hit/miss/store counters, memory retained per session, eviction, cross-conversation reuse and incremental TTFT. M43 incorporates an upstream APC redesign but keeps APC OFF; old zero-benefit findings do not establish the redesigned path's performance. |
| **Allocation lifetimes matter more than nominal KV size alone.** Growth can require simultaneous old/new buffers. | Full-cap preallocation addressed a measured reallocation OOM; short-generation probes could not characterize the full prefill peak. [Preallocation investigation](serving-path.md#kv_prealloc_tokens-structural-the-rule-stands) | Inspect the destination allocator and cache layout before choosing preallocation, paging or growth policy. Preserve full-cap preallocation in this stack; its exact policy is not a universal GPU recommendation. |
| **Diagnose non-convergence before choosing a lever.** Budget truncation, repetition, malformed tool calls and transport failure have different causes. | The resolved-budget correction recovered false passes; M20's old-profile loops were confounded by multiple settings. A matched 24K temperature ladder for `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-4bit` did not eliminate budget hits at any tested rung. [Budget resolution](metrics.md#the-resolved-thinking-budget-the-silent-08-clamp); [M20 and 2026-08-31 temperature-OFAT entries](campaign-results.md) | Hold task, seed, tune, cap and runtime fixed for a temperature OFAT; retain accuracy, convergence and token costs. Cross-model M11/M41 runaway counts cannot identify a temperature effect. |

Speculative algorithms may preserve a target distribution under their mathematical assumptions; that does not establish byte identity or implementation-wide losslessness under finite precision, truncation and stochastic sampling. This stack observed ON/OFF text differences and cross-restart divergence. M40's limited discordant outcomes do not prove that numerical noise is symmetric, nor do its small depth/vision gates prove equivalence across all prompts. Test the actual deployed predictor and sampler. ## Transfer protocol and remaining evidence

1. **Freeze provenance.** Record weights and predictor artifacts, conversion recipe, actual cache classes and quantization, source/dependency versions, template, resolved sampling/budget, cap, batching and concurrency. Separate old/new runtime tags. The previous fingerprint failures show why recording a field without observing its value is insufficient. [Fingerprint history](serving-path.md#the-suffix-decoding-saga-2026-08-16); [M43 specification](specs/upstream-2026-09-13.md)
2. **Validate the instrument cheaply.** Known-positive tests and five-item smokes precede expensive work. Treat HTTP failure as an infrastructure failure, and use derived timeouts without implicit retries. Smoke success screens gross regressions; it is not statistical recertification. [Campaign rules](../AGENTS.md); [M43 runtime validation](specs/upstream-2026-09-13.md#runtime-validation)
3. **Measure capacity, then quality OFAT.** Establish both picks' new-runtime capacity before M42; the second pick has no M41 capacity row. M42's fp16-KV failure is an estimate, not an observation. Its approved first decision is capacity at 262144; a verified gate failure ends that comparison with KV4 retained. The expanded quality arms need a separate scoped proposal. [M41 coverage note](campaign-results.md); [M42 specification](specs/upstream-2026-09-13.md#m42)
4. **Keep conclusions at the measured resolution.** Use matched budgets, explicit seeds, per-item failure causes and clustered intervals; preserve per-axis and per-language outcomes. Report task latency and output length alongside decode speed, and budget from pilot mean/max with tail allowance. Do not rank by a retry-based composite or call a small inconclusive experiment equivalent. [Metrics and statistical rulings](metrics.md); [pilot rule](../AGENTS.md)

Finalizing D14 requires M43 validation outcomes and an independent check of these claims. H200/B200 conclusions require actual destination-backend measurements; they remain outside this report's evidence. The current recommendation is to transfer this protocol and the cost hypotheses, while treating local capacity, tuning, predictor speed and model order as baseline observations to retest.
